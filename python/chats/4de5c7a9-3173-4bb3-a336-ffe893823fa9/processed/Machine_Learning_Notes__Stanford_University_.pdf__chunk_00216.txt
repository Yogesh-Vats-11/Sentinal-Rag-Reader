Other normalization layers. There are several other normalization layers that
aim to normalize the intermediate layers of the neural networks to a more
ﬁxed and controllable scaling, such as batch-normalization [Ioﬀe and Szegedy,
2015], and group normalization [Wu and He, 2018]. Batch normalization and
group normalization are more often used in computer vision applications
whereas layer norm is used more often in language applications.
