Upon visiting the sequence of states s0,s1,...with actions a0,a1,..., our

R(s0,a0) +γR(s1,a1) +γ2R(s2,a2) +···.
Or, when we are writing rewards as a function of the states only, this becomes
R(s0) +γR(s1) +γ2R(s2) +···.
